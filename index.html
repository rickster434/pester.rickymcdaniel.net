Features

Built to be ignored badly

Most reminders assume you will act the first time. Pester assumes you will swipe it away, get distracted and forget. So it comes back.

It escalates.

Three stages. A Poke is one quick bell. Snooze it, or just let it slide, and a Nudge rings and vibrates, 5 times. Keep it up and Harass takes over the lock screen with a full-screen alarm that keeps going. You choose how many snoozes each stage waits for. No cap. It stops when you tap Done.

Punishments.

Snoozing is something you will start to reconsider. Every poke you let slide counts as a snooze. Once: Pester wants a quick sum before you can tap Done. Twice: a word search. Three times or more: type ten real words of seven letters or more. The sound keeps going while you solve it. Five wrong answers and Pester lets you off the hook. On by default. You can switch them off in Settings, but where is the fun in that?

Quick add, always there.

Type the task in the bar at the bottom, or tap the mic and say it. Tap 15 m, 30 m, 1 h or Custom and send. It uses your defaults. Type "move laundry in 20m" or "call the vet at 6:30" and Pester picks the time.

Every option behind the +.

The + opens the full page: pick the date and time, repeat it daily, on weekdays, weekly on the days you choose, monthly or every few days, and set how each stage reaches you for this one pester.

Your sounds, your way.

Each stage gets its own Pester Type: quick bell, bell + vibrate, full-screen alarm + vibrate, or vibrate only. Pick its sound from a built-in library, soft ding to siren, use one from your phone, or record your own. Add a camera-light flash if you like.

Snooze without escaping.

Snooze 10 minutes or an hour, reschedule to a real time, or tap Forget it. Done and Snooze are on the notification and the ringing screen, so you never have to open the app in a hurry.

Quiet hours that don't quit.

Between the hours you set, pokes arrive silently and don't count as snoozes. They do not stop. They are waiting for the morning.

One screen.

Upcoming on top, Done below. Each section folds away and stays the way you left it. Done shows what you finished, how long it took and how many pokes it needed, and brings anything back with one tap. Light, Dark or Follow system.

How it works

Three steps, then it is Pester's problem

  1. Type it, tap a time.

    "What must get done?" Type or say it in the quick-add bar, tap 15 m, 30 m, 1 h or Custom, and send. Want a date, a repeat or a different escalation? Tap + instead.

  2. Get poked.

    At the time you set, a Poke. Every few minutes after that, another one, climbing to Nudge and then Harass the longer you leave it.

  3. Tap Done.

    That is the only thing that ends it. Snooze or reschedule if you honestly cannot right now. Just know that Pester counts every snooze.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What if I really can't do it right now?

Snooze it for 10 minutes or an hour, or reschedule it to a real time. Snoozing pauses the loop; it does not end it, and with Punishments on, every snooze raises the price of getting rid of it. Forget it ends it, no judgement.

What are Punishments?

Snoozing is something you will start to reconsider. Every poke you let slide counts as a snooze, whether you tap Snooze or just ignore it. Snooze once and Pester wants a quick sum before you can tap Done. Twice, a word search. Three times or more, you type ten real words of seven letters or more, and pasting is switched off. Get five answers wrong and Pester lets you off the hook. The sound keeps going while you solve it, and "Leave it for now" closes the puzzle, not the noise. Tap Done on the first poke and there is nothing to solve, silent pokes during quiet hours don't count, and a repeating pester starts clean each time. Punishments are on by default. Turn them off in Settings > Defaults and Done is one tap again.

Can I record my own alarm sound?

Yes. In any sound picker, or in Settings > Sounds, tap Record and record up to 10 seconds. Android asks for the microphone then, and only then; say no and everything else still works. The recording is saved on your phone and never leaves it. You can also use one of your phone's own sounds or an audio file. Your own sounds are not included in .pes backups, so pesters restored on another phone use a built-in sound instead.

Will it ring through Do Not Disturb?

Full-screen alarms already sound through most Do Not Disturb modes. For the rest, turn on "Ring through Do Not Disturb" in Settings > Reliability, which asks Android for the extra permission. It is off by default, and quiet hours let you silence pokes at night without stopping them.

Why does it ask for so many permissions?

Because nagging on time is hard on Android. Each one is listed below with the reason. The first-run walkthrough turns them on one after another, and Settings > Reliability shows which are granted with a Fix button for each. Pester has no reason to talk to the internet and does not.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics. Your pesters and any sounds you record live in Pester's own storage on the phone. Back up your pesters yourself to a .pes file in Settings > Data.

Permissions

Permissions in plain words

Each one is asked for when it is needed, with an explanation on screen first. The first-run walkthrough turns on the ones that keep Pester on time, and Settings > Reliability shows what is granted.

  • NotificationsEvery poke is a notification. Without this there is nothing to nag you with.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)A poke has to land at 7:30, not "sometime after". Pester uses exact alarms so it stays on time while the phone is dozing.
  • Full-screen notifications (Android 14 and newer)Lets Harass, or any stage you set to a full-screen alarm, open over the lock screen. Refuse it and Pester falls back to a loud heads-up notification.
  • Run at startupAfter a reboot Pester re-arms your active reminders.
  • Battery optimisation exemptionOptional. Some phones pause background apps and delay alarms; the walkthrough and Settings > Reliability offer the exemption and explain it.
  • Do Not Disturb accessOptional, only if you turn on Ring through Do Not Disturb.
  • MicrophoneOptional, and only asked when you tap Record to make your own alert sound. The recording is saved on this phone and never leaves it. Speaking a task uses your phone's own speech recogniser instead.
  • CameraNot requested. The flash option blinks the camera light, which needs no permission. Pester cannot see through the camera.
  • InternetNot requested. Pester has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play itself.
